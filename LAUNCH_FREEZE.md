# SCHOLARK Launch Freeze

Feature freeze is active for the public launch candidate.

Until the commercial launch is complete, changes to production should be limited to:
- launch-blocking bugs and regressions;
- security, privacy and account-safety fixes;
- payment correctness and billing-provider activation;
- reliability, performance and accessibility fixes;
- production configuration and monitoring.

New product features, experimental workspaces, Studio AI and Book Studio remain outside the launch candidate unless the freeze is explicitly lifted after launch.

Current remaining commercial dependency: Paddle production activation and a real-money end-to-end payment validation.
